What this sells

Your drivers know things your routing software doesn’t.

Bad addresses. Gate codes. Slow intersections. Aggressive dogs. Right now that knowledge lives in a group chat and disappears the next day.

This tool captures it — pinned to a map, tagged by type, searchable, and shared across your whole operation. Every driver’s hard-won route knowledge becomes a permanent asset instead of lost tribal knowledge.

Built for small DSP operators, last-mile contractors, and delivery teams running without enterprise tooling.

1Drop pins on stops that need attention — bad address, gate code, access note, hazard.
2Tag by type so drivers can filter to what matters on their specific route.
3Review queue surfaces flagged stops before dispatch so problems are caught, not discovered mid-route.
4Runs in any browser, no app install, no subscription. One-time build for your operation.
